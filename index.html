Case study

Fixing employee onboarding at a 4,000-person company

AI adoption case study with a process map, a product plan and a working prototype.

Open the case study↗

A fictional 4,000-person company where 40% of new hires start without a working laptop and 18% leave within 90 days. I mapped the current process, found the root causes, wrote a charter and roadmap, and built a prototype of a readiness portal.

Key points
  • It starts too late Nothing happens between the signed offer and day one.
  • Steps wait on each other Hand-offs that could run in parallel run one after another.
  • No owner No shared status and nobody in charge, so problems only show up on day one.
  • The fix Keep each team's rules, but change when information moves, who follows up and where status lives. Predictable beats fast.

Case study

A $30M long-only portfolio built on an AI thesis

A case study I wrote for an investment analyst hiring process.

Full case study↗

$30M in public stocks around an AI thesis, each held 1 to 10 years. The brief: beat the Nasdaq in up and down markets with 65 to 80% active share.

Key points
  • Seven themes Each theme is its own sub-portfolio, sized by conviction and by how closely it tracks the Nasdaq.
  • Correlation 0.41 to the Nasdaq over 3 years, 0.36 over the last 12 months and 0.57 in the 2022 drawdown.
  • Outside the US ASML, SK Hynix, Rheinmetall and Keyence fit the thesis but aren't in the index.
  • Themes the index misses Defence, space, robotics and biotech soften drawdowns without costing return.
  • Where AI helps Research on demand, news alerts, and the maths: active share, correlations, rebalancing and attribution.
  • Where it doesn't Building the thesis, talking to people close to the companies, and making the final call.
